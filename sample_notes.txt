Sample Physics Notes: Kinematics and Dynamics

1. Kinematics
Kinematics is the branch of mechanics that describes the motion of points, bodies, and systems without considering the forces that cause the motion.
Key equations for uniform acceleration (a = constant):
v = u + at
s = ut + (1/2)at^2
v^2 = u^2 + 2as
where u is initial velocity, v is final velocity, a is acceleration, s is displacement, and t is time.

2. Newton's Laws of Motion
First Law (Inertia): An object remains at rest or in uniform motion in a straight line unless acted upon by an external force.
Second Law: The rate of change of momentum of a body is directly proportional to the applied force. F = ma.
Third Law: For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

3. Work, Energy, and Power
Work is defined as force applied over a distance. W = F * d * cos(theta).
Kinetic Energy (KE) = (1/2)mv^2.
Potential Energy (PE) = mgh.
Power is the rate at which work is done. P = W/t.

4. Thermodynamics
The study of heat and temperature and their relation to energy and work.
First Law of Thermodynamics: Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed.
Second Law of Thermodynamics: The total entropy of an isolated system can never decrease over time.
